About Us

Sorsogon Animal Rescue and Adoption (SARA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need. Our mission is to provide a safe haven for abandoned, abused, and neglected animals, while promoting responsible pet ownership and animal welfare in our community.

We believe that every animal deserves a second chance at life and happiness. Through our rescue efforts, we aim to reduce the number of stray and homeless animals in Sorsogon City and surrounding areas.

We are SEC‑registered and accredited by local government units, and supports animal welfare through education, advocacy and promoting responsible pet ownership to prevent cruelty and neglect.

Our Mission & Vision



Mission

To protect animals ,promote their well-being, and help them find their forever home. Preventing cruelty, promoting compassion to animals, advocating for pet related issues and to educate the public about responsible pet ownership and animal welfare law.



Vision

To achieve through efforts the reduction of numbers of stray dogs and cats, cruelty and neglected. Also, to prevent rabies free and promote responsible pet owners
